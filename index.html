VOX for Mac

A music player that just sounds better. Minimalist design hides powerful features and support for every audio format you'll need.

Universal Format Support

Play lossy and lossless files effortlessly. Open M3U, PLS, XSPF, and CUE playlists. Access music on NAS devices and network drives. Export playlists in any format.

Effortless Control

Manage playback from the interface, Dock, or menu bar. Use keyboard media keys, headphone controls, or Apple Remote. Smart drop zones and Finder integration make adding tracks seamless.

Superior Audio Experience

Gapless playback, equalizer with presets, crossfade, and fade effects. Convert stereo to 5.1 or 7.1. Auto-fetch album art from MusicBrainz and Last.fm. HOG Mode and binaural rendering via Bauer DSP.

Seamless Integrations

Full SoundCloud support: stream, likes, and discovery. iTunes library, online radio, custom playlists. Last.fm scrobbling. AirPlay to any device. Auto-pause on headphone disconnect.

Focused Listening

System notifications, Dock previews, and Growl alerts keep you informed without distraction. Built for clarity, simplicity, and versatility on macOS.

How It Works

Step 1: Install and Launch

Step 2: Add Your Music

Step 3: Play and Customize

Auto-artwork, SoundCloud integration, and advanced effects elevate every track.
